Sources: a STAC /search (default Earth Search, Sentinel-2 L2A on AWS Open Data), a published starc store, the wildtiles inventory, a GDAL VRT mosaic, pasted COG URLs, an XYZ / WMTS tile server, or a Zarr store. The timeline under the map scrubs the days (drag it, or the arrow keys); days already read come back instantly. Pixels are read straight from the COGs (or tiles) by HTTP range request at the overview level that suits the region, warped onto one Web Mercator grid and mosaicked least-cloudy-first. Stretch, ramps, hillshade and composition happen here on the raw numbers. The URL hash is a permalink.

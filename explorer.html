Scenes come from a STAC /search (default Earth Search, Sentinel-2 L2A on AWS Open Data), a published starc store or the wildtiles inventory. The timeline under the map scrubs the days (drag it, or the arrow keys); days already read come back instantly. Pixels are read straight from the COGs by HTTP range request at the overview level that suits the region, warped onto one Web Mercator grid and mosaicked least-cloudy-first. Stretch and composition happen here on the raw numbers. The URL hash is a permalink.

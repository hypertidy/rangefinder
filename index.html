Scenes come from a STAC /search (default Earth Search, Sentinel-2 L2A on AWS Open Data) or the wildtiles inventory. Pixels are read straight from the COGs by HTTP range request at the overview level that suits the region, warped onto one Web Mercator grid and mosaicked least-cloudy-first. Stretch and composition happen here on the raw numbers. The URL hash is a permalink.
